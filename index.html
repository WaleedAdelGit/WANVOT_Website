Privacy Policy for WANVOT

Last updated: September 16, 2026

WANVOT ("the App") is a notes and voice-recording application designed to allow users to create, organize, protect, back up, and restore their notes and voice recordings.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use WANVOT.

1. Notes and Voice Recordings

WANVOT allows you to create written notes and record audio. Your notes and voice recordings are stored locally on your device. WANVOT does not require you to create an online account or upload your notes and recordings to a WANVOT server.

Microphone permission is used only to provide the voice-recording functionality when you choose to record audio.

2. Backup and Restore

WANVOT provides local backup and restore functionality. Backups are created and stored in a location selected or controlled through your device. WANVOT does not require an online WANVOT account or WANVOT cloud service to create or restore these backups.

You are responsible for protecting copies of backup files that you export, transfer, or store outside the App.

3. PIN and Protected Notes

WANVOT may provide PIN protection for the App and/or individual notes. Security features are intended to help prevent unauthorized access to protected content on your device. No security system can guarantee absolute protection against every possible form of unauthorized access.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

WANVOT may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may automatically collect or process certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and information related to advertisement performance, fraud prevention, and ad personalization, subject to your device settings, consent choices, applicable law, and the provider's policies.

WANVOT does not control information independently collected or processed by third-party advertising providers.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

Microphone: Used when you choose to record a voice note.

Internet and network access: May be used by advertising services and other functionality that requires network connectivity.

Advertising ID: Advertising services may use an advertising identifier where permitted by Android, your settings, consent choices, and applicable law.

WANVOT does not request access to your contacts, SMS messages, or precise location for its core note-taking and voice-recording functionality.

6. Data Sharing

WANVOT does not operate a server for storing your personal notes or voice recordings. Information may nevertheless be processed by third-party services integrated into the App, such as advertising providers, as described in this Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your notes or voice recordings.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Notes and voice recordings stored by WANVOT remain on your device until you delete them, remove the App and its associated local data, or otherwise erase the relevant data.

Backup copies that you create may remain in their backup location until you delete them. Information processed independently by third-party services is subject to those services' respective retention and deletion policies.

8. Security

WANVOT uses technical measures intended to protect sensitive locally stored information, including security mechanisms for protected notes where applicable. However, no application, device, or storage method can guarantee complete security.

You should keep your device secure and protect any backup files that you create or transfer.

9. Children's Privacy

WANVOT is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly operate a service designed to collect personal information from children. Advertising and other third-party services used by the App may have their own requirements and policies concerning children and age-related protections.

10. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Third-party services used by WANVOT, including Google advertising services, operate according to their own terms and privacy policies. You should review Google's Privacy Policy for information about Google's data practices.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when WANVOT's functionality, legal requirements, or third-party services change. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be revised.
